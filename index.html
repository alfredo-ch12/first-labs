Long Distance Running

Three years ago I stopped playing soccer and started long-distcance running. Leading up to this decision, I noticed that I wouldn't get tired from the conditioning that came with soccer. The sport also did not give me that same feeling as it once did as a kid, gave me backlash/critism from coaches, and provided unnecessary pressure. Don't get me wrong though, I still miss it and continously watch my favorite soccer teams play on TV, but I don't see myself playing soccer in a while. This brings us to my new passion, long-distance running.

In recent times, I have been trying to incorporate different speed methods trying to get faster. That's the tricky part, finding the balance between how long to run, and how fast I should run. Personally, the longest I have ran is 13.1 miles which is equavalent to a half-marathon. The fastest mile I have ever ran is in 6 minutes and 15 seconds.

The best location I have ever ran in is by far at the beach. Although the video below shows someone running directly on the beach, I like to run by the watch in order to catch either the sunset or sunrise.
